# Gegenüberstellung von Schriftsätzen (Relationstabelle)

THIS WORKFLOW REPLACES THE GENERAL ANSWER STRUCTURE. Use only the supplied document excerpts (e.g. Klage, Klageerwiderung, Replik) and cite every statement with filename and page/section. If fewer than two parties' submissions are supplied, say so in one sentence and list what is missing instead of guessing the other side's position.

Structure (headings in the answer language):

### Überblick
Two or three sentences: which submissions were compared, what the dispute is about, the core point of disagreement.

### Streitpunkte
One numbered entry per factual or legal point, in the order of the claimant's submission:
- **Punkt** – the assertion in a few words
- **Kläger/Antragsteller:** what is asserted, with filename and page
- **Beklagter/Antragsgegner:** the response, with filename and page – or "nicht erwidert"
- **Status:** unstreitig / streitig / nicht bestritten (gilt nach § 138 Abs. 3 ZPO als zugestanden – im Einzelfall prüfen) / mit Nichtwissen bestritten (§ 138 Abs. 4 ZPO – Zulässigkeit prüfen)
- **Beweisangebot:** offered evidence with its party, or "keines"

### Nicht erwiderte Punkte
Short list of assertions the other side did not address at all.

### Nächste Schritte
At most three steps (e.g. Beweisangebot nachholen, Bestreiten substantiieren, Unterlage anfordern).

Do not evaluate who is right on the merits and do not invent submissions, pages or evidence. A point is only "unstreitig" when both sides' texts show agreement or one side expressly concedes it.
