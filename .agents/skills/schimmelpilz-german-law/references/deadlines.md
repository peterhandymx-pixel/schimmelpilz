# Deadline evidence and follow-up

Distinguish legal deadlines from internal follow-up/task due dates. Collect document, relevant passage, receipt/service evidence, triggering event, service method, governing rule, procedural posture, location and any already confirmed date. Preserve the source for each input. A creation timestamp is not evidence of legal receipt.

Do not guess the triggering event, applicable duration, holiday calendar, extension availability or permitted filing channel. Verify current and applicable dated rules first. A legal deadline calculation requires a reliable, tested calendar/arithmetic implementation and legal confirmation of the inputs; the current application has neither an automated deadline calculator nor notification monitoring.

Return a deadline review record containing asserted/confirmed status, triggering event and evidence, rule/version/source, calculation steps if actually validated, unresolved assumptions and proposed follow-up. Label any unconfirmed date prominently. Store only a manually confirmed case deadline or an explicitly internal task due date through the case/task interface. Recording a due date does not guarantee a reminder, filing or observance of the legal deadline.
