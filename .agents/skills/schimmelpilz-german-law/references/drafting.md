# Draft documents

Choose correspondence, civil claim, application, objection or response according to the actual requested outcome and procedural route. A generic objection template does not establish that an objection is the available remedy. Confirm recipient and jurisdiction before producing a procedural draft; leave unresolved fields as clear placeholders.

Use the case's internal reference in the sender's reference field and a verified court/authority reference where one exists. A new court reference must not be fabricated from the internal case number. Preserve correct parties, procedural roles and addressee. Only use a lawyer's representation, title, signature block or approval when supported by the account/case and confirmed instructions.

For civil claims review the applicable [§ 253 ZPO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zpo/__253.html) and related current provisions for the necessary content and procedural form. Check court and party details, a sufficiently specified request, the supporting chronology, evidence tied to contested facts, value where relevant and identified exhibits. Check representation and permitted filing channel separately. Do not carry over numeric court thresholds from upstream examples.

For letters to authorities, courts, firms, companies or individuals, match salutation, requested action, factual explanation and attachment list to that recipient. Cite only inspected sources supporting the proposition. Keep research notes, unverified citations and internal review questions in a separate working note rather than hiding them in the outgoing text.

Return an editable draft plus a review checklist: missing facts, source support, adverse authority, procedural requirements, deadlines, amounts, evidence, recipient and signature. German and English outputs must preserve the meaning of legal terms; explain a term instead of pretending that another legal system's term is identical. The app currently saves and downloads text; automatic review, PDF/DOCX generation and dispatch are not implemented.
