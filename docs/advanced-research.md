# Extended local research

The source catalogue now supports 36 German federal statute collections. Added: UWG, UKlaG, ProdHaftG, BDSG, TzBfG, MiLoG, BetrVG, ArbZG, EntgFG, ZVG, EStG, UStG and AO. Their official XML downloads were fetched and parsed successfully on 2026-10-15. A configured collection is not necessarily downloaded: missing collections and failed refreshes are shown explicitly.

In **Recherche → Quelle gezielt suchen → Erweiterte Recherche**, combine keywords with a court prefix, decision date range, exact statute abbreviation and downloaded-original-only filter. Court or statute filters also work without keywords. Filters run in the local catalogue before the 50-result limit; search text is not sent to a public provider. Date filters apply to decisions, not historical statute applicability. Original texts can be added to the case and reviewed through the existing source/snapshot workflow.

Collection retrieval age is reported; more than 14 days or a failed refresh produces a warning. This is a technical retrieval-age policy, not assurance of legal currency. Current consolidated versions require a separate applicability check for the relevant date. Federal decision index records are searchable as metadata; substantive text search requires a downloaded original. No-result and limited-result views do not prove completeness. Existing missing/unknown citation warnings remain in chat and draft ledgers.

Coverage remains German federal law and the published federal decision index. EU, Mexico, state courts, paid commentaries, exhaustive case law and automatic legal approval are not implemented. Official links remain available for separate research.
